# Netflix Data Analysis Dashboard 📺

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built using the Netflix Titles dataset. The objective is to analyze Netflix's content catalog and identify trends, patterns, and insights related to movies, TV shows, ratings, genres, release years, and country-wise content distribution.

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand Netflix's content strategy and content growth over time.

---

## Business Problem

Netflix hosts thousands of movies and TV shows across multiple countries and genres. Understanding the distribution and trends of this content can help in:

- Identifying popular content categories
- Understanding content growth over the years
- Analyzing country-wise content production
- Examining rating distributions
- Supporting content acquisition and production decisions

## Key Insights

### 1. Movies Dominate Netflix
Movies represent the majority of Netflix's content library compared to TV Shows.

### 2. Significant Growth After 2015
Netflix experienced rapid content expansion after 2015.

### 3. United States Leads Content Production
The United States contributes the largest share of Netflix content.

### 4. TV-MA is the Most Common Rating
Mature audience content forms a significant portion of the catalog.

### 5. Drama and International Content are Highly Popular
Drama and International Movies appear among the most common genres.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Invest further in high-performing genres.
- Expand regional content production.
- Increase focus on international audiences.
- Analyze viewer engagement for top-rated categories.
- Continue diversification of content offerings.
